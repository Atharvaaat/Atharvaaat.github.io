    How I think

    Keep what you manage apart from what runs.

    In my agent platform, registration and versioning, runtime management and execution are separate services. Each can change, fail and scale without taking the others with it.

    Permission is a relationship, not a role.

    Who may execute, view, edit, delete or manage an agent follows from relationships between people, teams and resources. I check them with OpenFGA at the gateway.

    Every model speaks a dialect. A gateway should understand all of them.

    My LLM gateway work puts one interface over OpenAI- and Anthropic-style APIs, with routing and fallback between providers.

    Agents should be able to introduce themselves.

    I worked on A2A adapters and registry integration so agents built elsewhere can discover the agents on my platform and hand them work.
